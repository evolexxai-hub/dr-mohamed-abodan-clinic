How do I know if I am a candidate for rhinoplasty?

It starts with a consultation that looks at your nasal structure, skin thickness and breathing. Dr. Mohamed will tell you honestly what can and cannot be achieved.
How long is the recovery?

Recovery depends on the technique and on the patient. You will receive a clear, personalised follow-up plan at your consultation.
Will the result look natural?

The aim is balance and harmony with your own features, respecting ethnic characteristics instead of applying a fixed template.
Do you perform revision (redo) rhinoplasty?

Yes. Revision surgery starts with a full re-evaluation of the whole nasal structure, so the underlying cause is treated and not only the visible flaw.
